13 Oct 2026 · #horrorprompt #backroom #cyberpunk

I followed my shadow into the backroom. I had always wondered why we were bound together, but the truth hit me: he was severing our link to interface with a proper system.

A sudden terror struck—my entire world collapsed into darkness.

He stood in the server room, surrounded by archaic hardware from the dawn of AI.

I was on the verge of tears as he told me:
"Don't worry too much. You only found comfort in a false connection. Between us, it was never anything more than endless loops.”
